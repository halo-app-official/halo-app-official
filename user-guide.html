Quick Start Guide

What is Halo?

Halo is your automated safety net. It monitors your status silently in the background. If you fail to check in by your set time, Halo assumes you need help and automatically summons your trusted friends (your Archs).

1

Build Your Net

Go to the Archs tab and invite the people you trust most — friends, family, partners.

2

Set Your Timer

In Settings, choose how often you want to check in (e.g., every 24 hours).

3

Stay Green

As long as you timely check-in, your status remains Halo Green (Safe).

Part 2

Understanding Status Colors

Safe

Halo Green

You are safe and fully protected. Your check-in timer is running normally.

Warning

Halo Amber

Your check-in deadline is approaching (e.g., 1 hour remaining). This is a friendly reminder.

Open the app to reset your timer. No alerts are being prepared yet.
Grace Period

Halo Critical

You missed your check-in deadline. The final countdown has started (e.g., 10 minutes).

Immediate action required. Enter your PIN to stop the signal. Your Archs have not been notified yet.
Danger

Halo Red

The Critical timer ran out. A Signal has been sent to your entire Arch Network.

Part 3

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is an Arch?
An "Arch" is a pillar of your safety network. These are the specific people you authorized to receive alerts and view your status during an emergency.
How do I invite someone to be an Arch?
Go to the Archs tab and tap "Add Arch." They will receive an invitation request. Once they accept, they are linked to your safety net.
What happens if my phone battery dies?
Halo runs on a server-side timer. If your phone dies and you cannot check in, the timer will eventually run out on our server, and your Archs will be alerted. This ensures you are protected even if your phone is destroyed or out of battery.
What is Silent Mode?
Silent Mode allows you to disable alerts temporarily — perfect for movies, exams, or meetings.

Important: If the timer runs out in Silent Mode, NO signal is sent to your Archs. You are responsible for your own safety while Silent Mode is active.
I accidentally triggered an alert! What do I do?
Don't panic.
  • Open Halo immediately.
  • Enter your PIN to restore Halo Green.
  • The app will prompt you to send a message to reassure your Archs that you are okay.
What is the "Watchlist"?
The Watchlist is where you protect others. If you are an Arch for your friends or family, their status appears here. If everyone is safe, the list is green. If someone triggers an alert, they appear here instantly.
Part 4

Troubleshooting

My Arch didn't receive the notification

Solution 1: Ensure your Arch has accepted your invitation (check the Archs tab).

Solution 2: Ask your Arch to check their phone settings and allow "Notifications" for the Halo app.

Notifications are delayed or not showing

Check that battery optimization is disabled for Halo, and that the app is allowed to run in the background. On Android, set battery to "Unrestricted" for Halo.

Timer not resetting after check-in

Ensure you have a stable internet connection. The check-in requires a brief sync with our servers. Try closing and reopening the app.
