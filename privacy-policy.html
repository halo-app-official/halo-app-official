Privacy Policy

We believe safety shouldn't cost you your privacy. Here's exactly how we handle your data.

Last updated: February 2026
1

Data We Collect

  • Personal Identification: Name and Phone Number/Email — used solely for account creation and identification by your Archs.
  • Contact Information (Arch Network): The IDs/Contact details of the people you invite to be "Archs." This data is used only to connect your accounts within the app.
  • Device Tokens: Your device's unique Push Notification Token (FCM/APNs) to deliver alerts.
  • Usage Data: Check-in timestamps and current safety status (Green, Amber, Red).

NO LOCATION DATA: We do not collect, store, track, or transmit your GPS or precise location data. Alerts indicate status only (e.g., "User has missed a check-in"), not physical whereabouts.

2

How We Use Your Data

  • Safety Monitoring: To track your check-in timer and determine your current safety state.
  • Notification Delivery: To send "Halo Red" push notifications to your specific Arch Network if you fail to check in.
  • Account Linking: To ensure the right people (your Archs) receive your status updates.
3

Data Sharing

  • With Your Archs: Your safety status (Green/Red) and check-in history are shared only with the specific users you have authorized as "Archs."
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal data to outside parties for marketing or advertising.
4

User Control & Account Deletion

  • Revoking Access: You can remove an Arch at any time. Once removed, they immediately lose access to your status and history.
  • Direct Account Deletion: You may delete your account at any time, which permanently erases your data and Arch connections from our servers.
5

Data Security

  • Encryption: All communication between the app and our servers (status updates, invites) is encrypted via SSL/TLS.
  • PIN Protection: Your localized check-in PIN is stored securely on your device to prevent unauthorized status changes.
6

Children's Privacy

Halo is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at halo.app.official@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

7

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

Email: halo.app.official@gmail.com
