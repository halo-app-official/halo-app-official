Features

Why Halo?

Unlike traditional panic buttons, Halo works automatically if you can't reach your phone.

Smart Check-ins

Set a timer and live your life. As long as you're safe, your status stays Halo Green. Miss a check-in? Your Arch Network gets notified instantly.

Arch Network

Build your inner circle by inviting trusted friends and family to be your "Archs".

False Alarm Prevention

Before alerting anyone, Halo enters a grace period giving you time to confirm you're safe and prevent false alarms. You stay in full control of your safety narrative.

Silent Mode

Temporarily disable alerts for movies or meetings. You're always in control.

Live Watchlist

Be an Arch for others. See the real-time safety status of your friends and family in one centralized place.

Privacy First

Your PINs are encrypted. We protect your data as much as we protect you.

Process

Getting Started

Four simple steps to an automated safety net that works silently in the background.

01

Build Your Net

Add trusted friends and family to your Arch Network. They're the ones who will be notified if you need help.

02

Set Your Timer

Choose your check-in interval (e.g., every 24 or 36 hours). This is how often you'll need to confirm you're safe.

03

Stay Green

As long as you check in on time, your status stays Halo Green and everyone knows you're safe.

04

Automatic Protection

Miss a check-in? Halo goes into Critical mode. Don't respond? Your Archs get instantly notified.
