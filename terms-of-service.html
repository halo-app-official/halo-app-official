Description of Service

Halo is a personal safety monitoring application that operates on a timed check-in system. The Service allows users to:

  • Set a recurring check-in timer that runs server-side.
  • Designate trusted contacts ("Archs") who form a personal safety network.
  • Automatically alert Archs via push notification if the user fails to check in within the configured timeframe.
  • Monitor the safety status of people who have designated you as an Arch.

Halo is NOT an emergency service. It is a supplementary notification tool. It does not replace 911, police, ambulance, fire services, or any government emergency response system. In any life-threatening situation, always dial your local emergency number first.

Arch Network & Consent

The Arch system operates on explicit, mutual consent:

  • Sending a Request: You may send an Arch invitation to another registered Halo user. This does not grant you access to their data until they accept.
  • Accepting a Request: By accepting an Arch request, you agree to receive push notifications about that user's safety status, including emergency Halo Red alerts.
  • Revoking Access: Either party may remove the Arch connection at any time. Removal is immediate and irreversible — the removed Arch instantly loses all access to the other user's status and history.

Both users must be registered Halo users. You cannot add someone as an Arch unless they have the app installed and have created an account.
