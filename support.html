Common Issues

My Arch didn't receive a notification

Ensure your Arch has accepted your invitation first (check the Archs tab). Ask them to allow "Notifications" for Halo in their phone settings.

I accidentally triggered an alert

Open Halo immediately and enter your PIN to restore Halo Green. The app will prompt you to reassure your Archs with a message.

I want to delete my account

You can delete your account at any time from the app Settings. This permanently erases all your data and Arch connections from our servers.

Notifications are delayed or not showing

Check that battery optimization is disabled for Halo, and that the app is allowed to run in the background. On Android, also check "Unrestricted" battery settings for Halo.

Something else?

Email us at halo.app.official@gmail.com. We'll get back to you as soon as possible.

We typically respond within 24–48 hours. For urgent safety concerns, please contact your local emergency services immediately.
